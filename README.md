# FuseItAll

**Bring your Android phone and your Mac together.** FuseItAll is a free,
open-source app (AGPL-3.0) that links your Android phone to your Mac over your
local network — no accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions. Your data stays
between your two devices.

Two pieces work together:

- **FuseItAll (Android)** — an app you install on your phone.
- **FuseItAll (Mac)** — a Mac app you open on your computer.

Install both, pair them once with a simple on-screen code, and they start
working together.

---

## What you can do today

- **Simple QR pairing.** Open the Mac app, scan its code with your phone once,
  and confirm the security code. That's it. Unpair anytime with one tap.
- **See your phone on your Mac.** Once paired, your Mac shows your phone's
  name, model, and live battery level — including when it's charging.
- **Mirror your notifications.** Phone notifications (messages, calls, app
  alerts) show up on your Mac. Dismiss them from the Mac, and they clear on
  your phone too. You control it with a simple on/off switch.
- **Clipboard sync.** Copy text on your phone and paste it on your Mac — or
  the other way around. Choose the direction you want, from two-way syncing to
  one-way or off completely.
- **Sync your settings.** App preferences stay in sync between both devices
  automatically, so your phone and Mac always agree.
- **Browse and manage files.** View the files on your phone from your Mac, and
  add, change, or remove them.
- **View and manage photos.** Browse your phone's photos on the Mac, and
  add or delete them.

## What's coming next

The current app is a solid first step, we're building steadily
toward a much richer link between your phone and Mac:

- **Sync playback.** Sync your phone's playback state with the Mac.
- **Messages, everywhere.** Sync your text messages so you can view, send, and
  receive SMS from your Mac.
- **Polish everything.** Make everything feel polished and look great.
- **Full phone mirroring.** See your phone's screen on your Mac — and control
  it directly from there.

---

## Getting started

1. Install **FuseItAll (Mac)** and open it.
2. Install **FuseItAll (Android)** on your phone.
3. Make sure both devices are on the same network.
4. In the Mac app, start pairing to show a code.
5. On your phone, scan the code and confirm.

That's all it takes to get connected.
